About TechMate Academy

TechMate Academy is a self-study interface with beginner-friendly technology lessons. Content is educational and may not cover every device, operating system or professional standard. Follow trusted guidance for security-sensitive tasks.

Privacy: this version saves your learning information in this browser. It does not create an account or send your notes to a server. Clearing browser data may remove saved information. Advertising spaces are placeholders only; no ad service is connected.
